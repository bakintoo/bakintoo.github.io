Privacy Policy

Needle in the Haystack Sim, by Jambo Games
Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Needle in the Haystack Sim (the "Game") on Android, why, and the choices you have. The Game is made by Jambo Games ("we", "us").

The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. There is no sign-in.
  • Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The Game is free and shows ads. Our ad partner, Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), and the ad networks it works with collect some device information to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the Game's Settings.
  • Every rewarded ad is optional. There are no in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (your current game, records and collection) and your settings (language, sound, graphics quality and controls) in the Game's own storage on your device. We do not receive this information, and it is deleted when you uninstall the Game. The Settings screen can also reset your current game or copy your save as text, which stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

Information collected for advertising

The Game uses Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource), a service of Unity Technologies, to show rewarded and interstitial ads. LevelPlay may load ads from ironSource Ads and Unity Ads, and from other ad networks we may add later (they will be listed here). When an ad is requested or shown, these services may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID) and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and the approximate location it reveals (country or city, not precise location);
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system, language, screen size, network type and the Game's version;
  • information about the ads, such as which ads were shown, whether you watched them to the end or tapped them.

They use this information to show ads, to measure how ads perform, to cap how often you see the same ad, to detect fraud and, where you have allowed it, to personalise ads. We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos or files, and the Game does not ask for those permissions.

How these companies handle data is described in their own policies:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads): unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Your choices

  • Ad personalisation consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): the first time you open the Game we ask whether ads may be personalised. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests. You can change your answer at any time under Settings > Ad choices in the Game.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of interest-based ads, in your phone's Settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads, depending on the phone).
  • US residents: we do not sell your personal information for money. Ad networks may use it as described above; you can limit this by deleting or resetting your advertising ID.
  • Deleting your data: uninstalling the Game deletes everything it stored on your device. For information held by the ad services, use the choices above or contact them through their privacy policies; you can also contact us and we will help.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests are best directed to the ad services listed above, but we are happy to help.

Legal basis (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Personalised ads are shown only with your consent. Non-personalised ads, measurement and fraud prevention rely on our legitimate interest in funding a free game.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will work with our ad partner to delete it.

International transfers and security

Our ad partners may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, under the safeguards described in their policies. Information sent to them is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's store listing.
